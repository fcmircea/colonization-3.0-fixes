# Romanian glyphs for the Colonization fonts (draft 1, Claude, 2026-09-27).
# Block: [FONTNAME letter], then one line per pixel row (exactly the font height).
# '.' clear, '1' main colour, '2' shadow, '3' edge. All rows of a glyph: same width.
# Method copied from MicroProse's German fonts: mark on top rows for lowercase;
# capitals with a mark above are squashed by two rows (German Ä/Ü); the comma of
# ș/ț goes in the row under the baseline. Slot bytes: see SLOTS in fontedit.py.
# FONTINTR drop shadow (revised 2026-09-29, as the German ä/ö/ü): every main
# pixel '1' of a mark gets '3' to its right and '2' below it, except where the
# shadow would land on the letter body (rows 0-1 hold the mark, the body starts
# at row 2). The comma of ș/ț/Ș/Ț is one '1' with '3' to its right in row 8.
# Â Î: mark in rows 0-1, body squashed into rows 2-6, so the letter keeps the
# full cap height; Î is drawn like î (in this font I and l are one bar too).
# Ă: a solid bar in row 0, its shadow in row 1, over the full-height A (Florin's
# pick 5, 2026-09-29). A green cup on the A's apex reads as X (tester S11); a
# brown cup vanishes on wood and on the highlight bar, leaving Ä (Florin's
# pirate-dialogue screenshot). FONTTINY ă/Ă: solid 3-px bar, one clear row,
# body; tiny Ă sits 1 px low so the A keeps its pointed top (was read as H). Two
# dots read as a broken top / German ä (Florin's shape, 2026-09-29).
# FONTTINY Î: open hat, one clear row, 3-px stem ending 1 px below the line;
# FONTTINY î: the same with a 2-px stem on the line, so Î is î plus one pixel at
# the foot (Florin's pair P2, 2026-09-29; hat touching the stem read as short, a
# base serif read as "2", S13).
# FONTTINY ț: t without its foot, one clear row, comma (Florin's pick ț1). FONTTINY
# â: ă with the bar turned into an open hat, body 1 px low (Florin's pick â1).
# FONTTINY Â: right half of the hat over a 4-row A, 1 px low, as Ă (Florin's pick
# Â8); before, tiny Â was pixel for pixel the same as A.
